My research brings together biomedical signal processing and medical AI to support reliable cardiovascular monitoring, with particular attention to preserving ECG morphology.

## Deep Learning for ECG Reconstruction

**Research question:** How can a 12-lead ECG be reconstructed from a limited number of leads while preserving the shape and clinically relevant features of the signal?

I focus on deep learning-based ECG reconstruction, morphology preservation, and reconstruction quality assessment. This is the main focus of my doctoral research at Politecnico di Milano.

## Biomedical Signal Processing

**Research question:** How can fetal ECG signals be extracted from abdominal recordings affected by interference and noise?

My work in this area includes non-invasive fetal ECG extraction, signal decomposition, denoising, and analysis, using both signal processing methods and deep learning.

## Future Research Interests

I am interested in extending my work to **EEG signal processing** and **multimodal physiological signal analysis and fusion**. These are directions I would like to explore, including how complementary physiological signals can be analyzed together.
